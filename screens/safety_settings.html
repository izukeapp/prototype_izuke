How Izuke keeps you safe
Before a meetup, share a live link with someone you trust. They can follow your location until it ends, no app needed.
During a meetup, hold SOS to call the emergency number and text your contacts your live link.
Set up your phone's Emergency SOS: on iPhone, Settings → Emergency SOS.
Izuke doesn't monitor your safety. The people you share with do — make sure they know to call you if something looks wrong.
